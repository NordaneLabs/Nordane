Our journey with Okam

### From the first line to launch in four months.

In July 2026 we started rebuilding Okam from the ground up. By October it runs the till, the kitchen, the online shop and the marketing, with AI built in.

July – August 2026

#### The till

We started where a restaurant makes its money: a new till for iPad and the back office behind it.

- Till (POS) for iPad
- Card terminals and receipt printers
- Norwegian certified cash register rules
- Back office for menu, staff and money
- Tripletex accounting link

September – October 2026

#### The whole restaurant

Then everything around the till, on the same base and the same data.

- Kitchen display
- Online ordering, web and app
- Table reservations
- Staff planning
- Gift cards, Wolt and Foodora

October 2026

#### Factory and launch

Now the platform improves itself every day and goes to market.

- Our software factory: problem reports become tested fixes, automatically
- Ask Okam, the AI assistant for owners
- The new okam.no website
- Switzerland: CHF, German and TWINT

- **4** months from start to launch
